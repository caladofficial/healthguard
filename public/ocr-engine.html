Document AI · 2

Difficult Photo OCR

Real reports arrive folded, faded, glared, skewed and scribbled on. The engine is designed for that reality — and it knows when it does not know.

Blur & glare handlingHandwritingOCR ensembleConsensus + confidence

Enhancement

Before a single character is read

Ensemble

OCR-A · OCR-B · OCR-C → consensus

Three readers, one verdict

Independent OCR engines transcribe the same enhanced image; a consensus layer reconciles them; medical validation then checks plausibility of test names, units and ranges.

Low confidence stops the line

A value that cannot be trusted is flagged for the Clinical Review Queue instead of becoming structured data.

Difficult cases

The benchmark we design against

  • Low resolution and heavy compression
  • Mobile photos with glare and shadows
  • Handwritten marks, stamps, signatures
  • Overwritten and crossed-out text
  • Folds, skew, torn pages
  • Multi-page documents and tables
  • Mixed languages — English, Hindi, Hinglish
  • Regional laboratory formats and layouts

Every extracted field carries confidence and provenance. Uncertain OCR never silently becomes trusted medical data.
