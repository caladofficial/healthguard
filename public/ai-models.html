Document AI · 4

Eight specialists, not one giant

Foundation models do the heavy lifting; specialist models own each clinical job; every model has its own evaluation and version.

Foundation + fine-tunePer-model evalsVersioned registry

Suite

Model inventory

◇

Model 1 — Classifier

CBC, LFT, KFT, Lipid Profile, Prescription, Discharge Summary, Radiology, Pathology, ECG, Unknown.

◎

Model 2 — OCR

Specialized document OCR with consensus ensemble support.

⬡

Model 3 — Medical NER

TEST, VALUE, UNIT, REFERENCE_RANGE, MEDICATION, DOSAGE, DIAGNOSIS, SYMPTOM, BODY_PART, DATE, DOCTOR, HOSPITAL.

∞

Model 4 — Normalization

Maps Hb / Hemoglobin / HGB to one concept — LOINC, UCUM, ICD, RxNorm.

▤

Model 5 — Patient summarizer

Cautious, understandable explanations. No diagnosis language.

▤

Model 6 — Doctor summarizer

Findings, abnormalities, historical changes, follow-up items, missing information.

◉

Model 7 — Triage model

Recommends workflow urgency only. Never a disease claim.

▣

Model 8 — Safety model

Unsupported conclusions, contradictions, hallucinations, dangerous advice, missing context, uncertainty.

Strategy

Do not train a foundation model from scratch

  1. 01 Vision model
  2. 02 Document understanding
  3. 03 Medical NLP
  4. 04 Structured data
  5. 05 Clinical RAG / reasoner
  6. 06 Safety validator
  7. 07 Doctor-facing result

First use existing foundation models + specialized models. Then fine-tune specific components.
